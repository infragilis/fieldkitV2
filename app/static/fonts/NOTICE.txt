Self-hosted webfonts
====================

app/static/fonts/*.woff2 are vendored builds of the following typefaces,
served locally by the Fieldkit appliance so no third-party font service is
contacted at runtime:

- Inter (weights 400-700) — SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Space Grotesk (weights 600-700) — SIL Open Font License 1.1
- JetBrains Mono (weights 400-600) — SIL Open Font License 1.1

Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, reproduced in
full in OFL.txt (see https://scripts.sil.org/OFL for the canonical text and FAQ).

They were fetched once from Google Fonts (which only redistributes the original
OFL-licensed projects) and committed here. See app/static/fonts.css for the
@font-face rules.
